How the automatic recommendation works

Two scores of 4 or 5 in a row on the same session move Milo to the next harder session. Two scores of 1 or 2 in a row move him to an easier session. Any other score pattern repeats the current session.

When Should Milo Move Up?

Move up one session when:

  • He scores 4 or 5 twice in a row.
  • Both scores are for the same length and level.
  • He can still take treats.
  • His crying stays brief and manageable.

Repeat or move down when:

  • Two scores of 1 or 2 occur in a row.
  • His crying becomes more intense.
  • He cannot settle even when you return.
  • He scratches frantically or refuses food.

Helpful reminder

A small amount of crying does not mean the session failed. Record what happened and follow the recommendation shown for the next session.
